Cookie Policy

Website: pbnskye.com · Effective: March 2026 · Transparency & Ad Standards

This is the Cookie Policy for PBN Skye, accessible from https://pbnskye.com. We believe in complete transparency regarding how and why cookies and local browser storage are utilized when you access our technical tools and educational articles.

1. What Are Cookies?

As is common practice with almost all professional websites, this site uses cookies, which are tiny files that are downloaded to your computer or mobile device, to improve your experience. This page describes what information they gather, how we use it, and why we sometimes need to store these cookies. We will also share how you can prevent these cookies from being stored, however this may downgrade or break certain elements of the site's functionality.

2. The Cookies We Set

  • Preferences & Interface State: In order to provide you with an optimal browsing experience, we provide the functionality to set your preferences for how this site runs (such as our Light/Dark mode toggle). In order to remember your preferences across page visits, we store your choice in browser localStorage or session cookies so this information is recalled automatically.
  • Form Processing Cookies: When you submit data to through a form such as those found on our Contact page, cookies or anti-spam tokens may be set to remember your user details for future correspondence and to prevent automated bot submissions.

3. Third-Party Advertising Cookies (Google AdSense)

In some special cases, we also use cookies provided by trusted third parties. The following section details which third-party cookies you might encounter through this site:

  • Google AdSense: We use Google AdSense to serve contextual and interest-based advertisements to help support our free tools. The Google DoubleClick DART cookie is used to serve more relevant ads across the web and limit the number of times that a given ad is shown to you.
  • For more information on Google AdSense, see the official Google AdSense privacy and terms FAQ at https://policies.google.com/technologies/ads.

4. How to Disable and Manage Cookies

You can prevent the setting of cookies by adjusting the settings on your browser (see your browser Help for how to do this). Be aware that disabling cookies will affect the functionality of this and many other websites that you visit. Disabling cookies will usually result in also disabling certain functionality and features of this site. Therefore, it is recommended that you do not disable cookies.

Browser-Specific Cookie Management:

  • Google Chrome: Settings > Privacy and security > Third-party cookies > Block or customize cookie permissions.
  • Mozilla Firefox: Settings > Privacy & Security > Enhanced Tracking Protection > Custom.
  • Apple Safari: Preferences > Privacy > Manage Website Data > Prevent cross-site tracking.
  • Microsoft Edge: Settings > Cookies and site permissions > Manage and delete cookies and site data.

5. Opting Out of Targeted Advertising

You may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting:

  • Google Ads Settings
  • Digital Advertising Alliance (DAA) Consumer Choice
  • Your Online Choices (Europe / EDAA)

6. More Information

Hopefully that has clarified things for you. As was previously mentioned, if there is something that you aren't sure whether you need or not, it's usually safer to leave cookies enabled in case it does interact with one of the features you use on our site.

If you are still looking for more information, you can contact us through our email at support@pbnskye.com or visit our Contact page.
